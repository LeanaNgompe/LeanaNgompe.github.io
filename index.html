About Me

I study how social platforms and generative AI amplify existing power asymmetries, using mixed-methods research grounded in qualitative and discourse analysis, and I'm building toward computational methods. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Columbia University and a B.A. in Computer Science and Women's, Gender, and Sexuality Studies from Barnard College, and I'm applying to PhD programs.
